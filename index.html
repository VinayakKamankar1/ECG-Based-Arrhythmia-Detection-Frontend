11 Recognized Cardiac Patterns
Comprehensive multi-pattern detection trained against international benchmarks
NSRNormal Risk
Normal Sinus Rhythm
Regular rhythm originating at the SA node. Heart rate typically stable within 60–100 bpm range.
SBRMild Risk
Sinus Bradycardia
Resting heart rate below 60 bpm. Frequently physiologic in athletic cohorts; evaluate for conduction delays.
STachMild Risk
Sinus Tachycardia
Sinus rate exceeding 100 bpm. Typical physiological response to adrenergic trigger, infection, or volume loss.
PACMild Risk
Premature Atrial Contraction
Ectopic atrial focus firing before anticipated sinus beat. Narrow QRS; monitor burden if symptomatic.
PVCModerate Risk
Premature Ventricular Contraction
Ectopic focus originating within ventricular myocardium. Characterized by wide, bizarre QRS complex and full compensatory pause.
BigeminyModerate Risk
Ventricular Bigeminy
Repetitive alternating pattern of normal sinus beat followed immediately by a PVC (N-V-N-V pairing).
SVTModerate Risk
Supraventricular Tachycardia
Paroxysmal rapid rhythm arising above the bundle branches. Regular RR intervals; rate commonly 150–220 bpm.
AFibHigh Risk
Atrial Fibrillation
Chaotic, disorganized atrial depolarizations without discernible P waves. Highly irregular ventricular response and variable RR intervals.
VTHigh Risk
Ventricular Tachycardia
Three or more consecutive ventricular beats at a rate >100 bpm. Critical hemodynamically unstable clinical presentation.
BlockModerate Risk
Conduction / AV Heart Block
Impaired electrical propagation between atria and ventricles. Variable PR prolongation or non-conducted P waves.
PacedMonitored
Electronic Paced Rhythm
Artificial pacemaker depolarization artifact followed by ventricular response. Baseline tracking.
